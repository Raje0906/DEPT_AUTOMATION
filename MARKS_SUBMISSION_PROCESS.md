# Department Examination & Marks Submission Process

A step-by-step guide explaining how marks are entered, reviewed, approved, and converted into official SPPU grades in the Department Automation Portal.

---

## Quick Summary Flow

1. **Course Allocation:** HOD assigns faculty to classes and subjects.
2. **Marks Entry:** Faculty enters marks (or uploads CSV) and saves as **Draft**.
3. **Submission:** Faculty submits marks to the HOD (the marksheet gets **Locked** for faculty).
4. **HOD Verification:** HOD inspects class performance:
   * **Approve:** Moves to **Approved** status.
   * **Send Back:** Reverts to **Draft** with remarks so the teacher can make corrections.
5. **Publishing:** HOD publishes results; students can now see their grade cards and SGPA.

---

## 1. Access Control (Who can enter marks?)

* The Head of Department (HOD) assigns subjects to teachers for specific classes and academic years (for example: *Prof. Smith teaches Operating Systems for TE Comp 1*).
* **Security Rule:** A faculty member can **only** enter marks for the specific classes and subjects assigned to them.
* If a faculty member attempts to access any other class or subject, the system automatically blocks access.

---

## 2. The 8 Examination Components

The department tracks 8 different exam types throughout the semester:

| Exam Type | Max Marks | Counts in Final SPPU Result? | What It Is Used For |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Unit Test 1** | 30 | No | Internal assessment & practice test |
| **Unit Test 2** | 30 | No | Internal assessment & practice test |
| **In-Sem Exam (CCE)** | 30 | **Yes** | Mid-semester theory examination |
| **Mock Theory Exam** | 70 | No | Practice exam before the final university theory paper |
| **Mock Practical Exam** | 25 | No | Practice exam before the university practical/oral |
| **Term Work (TW)** | 25 | **Yes** | Continuous assessment during the semester |
| **Final Practical / Oral** | 25 | **Yes** | Official University practical or oral exam |
| **End-Sem Theory Exam** | 70 | **Yes** | Official University final theory exam |

---

## 3. How Term Work (TW) Is Calculated

For practical subjects, the **25 Marks** for Term Work are calculated from 4 parameters:

* **Attendance:** Up to 5 marks (0 to 5)
* **Assignment 1:** Up to 7 marks (0 to 7)
* **Assignment 2:** Up to 7 marks (0 to 7)
* **Timely Submission:** Up to 6 marks (0 to 6)

**Formula:**
> **Total Term Work (out of 25)** = Attendance + Assignment 1 + Assignment 2 + Timely Submission

---

## 4. How Faculty Enter Marks

Faculty members open the **Marks Entry** screen from their portal:

1. **Student List:** Students are automatically listed in order of their roll number.
2. **Entering Marks:**
   * Faculty type marks directly into the table.
   * If a student was absent, clicking the **Absent** checkbox automatically sets marks to 0 and records the absent status.
   * The system prevents invalid inputs (e.g., negative numbers or marks higher than the allowed maximum).
3. **Bulk Excel / CSV Upload:**
   * Instead of typing manually, faculty can download a pre-filled CSV template, enter marks in Excel, and upload the file in one click.
4. **Saving as Draft:**
   * Clicking **"Save Marks"** saves entries as **Draft**.
   * While in Draft status, faculty can return and change marks anytime.

---

## 5. Submitting to HOD for Approval

1. When all student marks for the class are ready, the faculty clicks **"Submit to HOD for Approval"**.
2. **Status Changes:** Marks change from **Draft** to **Submitted**.
3. **Locking:** Once submitted, the marksheet is locked for the faculty member so no accidental changes happen while the HOD reviews it.

---

## 6. HOD Review & Decision

The HOD opens the review dashboard to check class performance:

* The HOD checks class averages, absent counts, top scorers, and failing students.
* **The HOD has two options:**
  1. **Approve:** Marks status changes to **Approved**. The marksheet is officially confirmed and locked.
  2. **Send Back for Correction:** The HOD enters mandatory remarks explaining what needs to be fixed. The marksheet unlocks and returns to **Draft** status so the faculty member can correct and resubmit it.

---

## 7. Result Publishing & Grade Calculation

When all subjects for a semester/class are approved, the HOD clicks **"Publish Results"**.

### A. How Subject Totals Are Calculated

* **Theory Subjects (Total 100 Marks):**
  * In-Sem Exam (30 Marks) + End-Sem Exam (70 Marks) = **100 Marks**
* **Practical Subjects (Total 50 Marks):**
  * Term Work (25 Marks) + Final Practical/Oral (25 Marks) = **50 Marks**
* **Seminar (Total 50 Marks):**
  * Term Work = **50 Marks**

---

### B. SPPU 10-Point Grading Scale

The student's percentage is converted into a grade and grade points according to SPPU rules:

| Percentage Range | Letter Grade | Grade Points | Performance Level |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **80% to 100%** | **O** | 10.0 | Outstanding |
| **70% to 79%** | **A+** | 9.0 | Excellent |
| **60% to 69%** | **A** | 8.0 | Very Good |
| **55% to 59%** | **B+** | 7.0 | Good |
| **50% to 54%** | **B** | 6.0 | Above Average |
| **45% to 49%** | **C** | 5.0 | Average |
| **40% to 44%** | **P** | 4.0 | Pass |
| **Below 40%** | **F** | 0.0 | Fail |

---

### C. How SGPA and CGPA Are Calculated

* **SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average):**
  > **SGPA** = Total of (Each Course's Credits × Grade Points Earned) ÷ Total Credits in that Semester

  *Example:*
  If a student earns:
  * Course 1 (3 Credits): Grade A (8.0 Points) = 24
  * Course 2 (4 Credits): Grade B+ (7.0 Points) = 28
  * Total Credits = 7
  * SGPA = (24 + 28) ÷ 7 = **7.43**

* **CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average):**
  > **CGPA** = Total Grade Points Earned across all Semesters ÷ Total Credits across all Semesters

---

## 8. What Students See

Once published:
* Results become visible in the **Student Portal**.
* Students can view their marks breakdown, grades, SGPA, and download their official digital grade cards.
