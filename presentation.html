Point 1 Problem Statement: Academic Evaluation Challenges

Manual spreadsheet tabulation fails to handle autonomous engineering complexity

📊
8-Exam Heads Complexity
Students are evaluated across In-Sem (30M), End-Sem (70M), Term Work (25M), Oral/Practical (25M), and Internal tests. Compiling these in isolated Excel files introduces human computation delays of 3 to 4 weeks.
⚖️
Subjective Condonation
Statutory University rules like SPPU Ordinance 0.4 permit up to 3 grace marks for marginal fail scores (37-39). In manual spreadsheets, borderline cases are frequently missed or awarded without an auditable trail.
📉
Evaluator Disparity
Different faculty assessing different divisions (Div 1 vs. Div 2) leads to unmoderated grading variance (12–15% mean disparities), leaving students subject to evaluator bias without statistical checks.

Point 1 Problem Statement: Departmental Operational Silos

Academic activities and student progress tracking operate in disconnected tools

📁
Fragmented Project & Seminar Workflows
BE Project topic preferences, guide allocation, and TE Seminar stage review rubrics are scattered across disparate Google Forms, email threads, and loose paper evaluation sheets.
⏱️
Delayed Attendance Intervention
Statutory university attendance (<75%) is typically tabulated at semester-end. Lack of timely month-by-month triage prevents early remediation and leaves students with sudden exam detentions.
🔒
Zero Cryptographic Verification
Standard printouts and PDFs lack digital signatures and tamper-proof verification mechanisms, making issued grade cards vulnerable to forgery and unauthorized modifications.

Point 2 Motivation: Institutional Transformation

Driving academic autonomy, error-free governance, and rapid result turnaround

🚀
Transition to College Autonomy
  • Autonomous institutions must conduct the entire examination board lifecycle internally.
  • Requires instantaneous, 100% mathematically verified SGPA and CGPA computation following university credit patterns.
  • Eliminates administrative backlogs, reducing publication turnaround from 25 days to under 1 hour.
🛡️
Statutory Fairness & Student Protection
  • Guaranteeing that every eligible student receives Ordinance 0.4 condonation automatically.
  • Early detection of ATKT and Year-Down risks enables proactive academic counseling before progression gates lock.
  • Guarantees parity and fairness across divisions through automated statistical moderation alerts.

Point 3 Project Objectives: Core Engineering Goals

Clear, quantifiable, and outcome-based engineering deliverables

OBJECTIVE 01
Multi-Exam Ingestion
Build an automated mark compilation engine supporting 8 exam types with boundary sanity checks and absentee disambiguation.
OBJECTIVE 02
Ordinance 0.4 Condonation
Implement an autonomous rule engine for statutory university grace marks and ATKT/detention progression classification.
OBJECTIVE 03
Statistical Moderation
Implement algorithms (Mean μ, StdDev σ, IQR) to detect inter-divisional evaluator bias before grade locking.
OBJECTIVE 04
Attendance Defaulter Triage

Department Lifecycle Hub
Unify BE Project allocations, TE Seminar rubrics, Student Clubs, and Department Newsletters into a single portal.
OBJECTIVE 06
Cryptographic Verification
Generate SHA-256 digital digests with dynamic QR-code verification on published grade sheets with full audit logging.

Point 4 Feasibility Study: Technical & Operational

Rigorous assessment of project viability and implementation readiness

⚡
Technical Feasibility
  • Full-Stack Architecture: Built on industry-standard Node.js (Express), React + Vite, and Tailwind CSS.
  • ACID Relational Integrity: PostgreSQL guarantees strict transaction isolation, foreign keys, and rollbacks on mark mutations.
  • Instantaneous Execution: SGPA, CGPA, and statistical metrics run in sub-100ms without requiring heavy GPU compute.
👥
Operational Feasibility
  • Role-Tailored Portals: Zero training overhead; intuitive interfaces for HOD, Faculty, Students, and Class Teachers.
  • Excel-Compatible: Faculty can continue using offline Excel sheets via 1-click CSV template download and upload.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Gate: Autonomous checks flag anomalies, but final publishing authority remains strictly with the HOD.

Point 4 Feasibility Study: Economic & Legal

Cost advantages and regulatory compliance

💰
Economic Feasibility
  • 100% Open-Source Stack: Built using React, Node.js, and PostgreSQL with zero recurring licensing fees.
  • Massive Institutional Savings: Saves tens of thousands of rupees compared to commercial enterprise ERP subscriptions ($10,000+/yr).
  • Low Hardware Footprint: Efficiently runs on existing college laboratory servers or low-cost virtual private servers (2 vCPU, 4GB RAM).
📜
Legal & Regulatory Feasibility
  • SPPU Ordinance Alignment: Faithfully enforces autonomous university passing criteria and Ordinance 0.4 condonation rules.
  • NBA & NAAC Alignment: Supports Outcome-Based Education (OBE) with transparent rubrics and audit trails.
  • Data Privacy & Security: Enforces server-side RBAC, encrypted credentials (bcrypt), and single-use password reset tokens.
